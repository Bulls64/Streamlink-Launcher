Live, minus the browser.

Streamlink Launcher opens Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Dailymotion and Steam TV in VLC, mpv or PotPlayer. Streamlink Chat keeps Twitch, Kick and Steam TV chats in a window alongside. Same style, same lightness, no dependencies.
